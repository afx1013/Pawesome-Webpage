About Pawesome

Dedicated to giving every animal a second chance at happiness

Our Story

Founded in 2015, Pawesome began with a simple mission: to connect loving families with pets in need of homes. What started as a small grassroots effort has grown into a thriving animal rescue organization serving our entire community.

Every year, we rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome hundreds of dogs, cats, and other animals. Our dedicated team of volunteers and staff work tirelessly to ensure each animal receives the medical care, training, and love they deserve while they wait for their forever homes.

Our Mission

To rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome animals in need while promoting responsible pet ownership and building a compassionate community.
